About the Project

This platform is designed to help users find and understand climate policy documents and research papers using Natural Language Processing, semantic search, retrieval and language models.

What the Platform Can Do

Question Answering

Ask questions in natural language and retrieve relevant information.

Semantic Search

Find documents based on meaning, not only exact keywords.

Summarization

Turn lengthy policy documents into shorter explanations.

Policy Comparison

Compare important points across different climate policies.

Document Classification

Organize documents by climate topics and policy areas.

Research Support

Help students and researchers explore climate-related information.
