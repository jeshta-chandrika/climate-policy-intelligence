About the Project

This project uses Natural Language Processing and modern AI techniques to help users find useful information from climate policy documents and research papers.

The platform supports semantic search, document classification, summarization, question answering and policy comparison.

Key Features

Policy Search

Find relevant climate policies and documents using keywords and semantic search.

Document Classification

Organize documents according to climate topics and policy areas.

Summarization

Get short and clear summaries from lengthy climate documents.

Question Answering

Ask questions and retrieve information from available documents.

Policy Comparison

Compare important points from different climate policies.

Research Insights

Explore research information and identify useful climate-related topics.
